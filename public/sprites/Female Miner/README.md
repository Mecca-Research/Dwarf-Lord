# Female Miner

**Role:** Dark-haired female miner design; Nessa, Mora and Yara currently share it.

**Age:** Adult.

**Identity:** Stocky woman; no facial hair; long dark brown hair; iron helmet; ochre tunic and brown leather vest; broad forearms and boots.

**Consistency:** Do not replace with Helga or a bearded generic miner. Preserve dark hair and ochre costume.
