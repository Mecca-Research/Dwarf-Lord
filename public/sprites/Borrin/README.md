# Borrin

**Role:** Senior manager, consultant and keeper of the ledger, retained from the previous owner.

**Age:** Second oldest dwarf; only Elder is older.

**Identity:** Receding combed iron-grey hair with dark temples; angular weathered face; straight nose; bronze reading spectacles; neat two-ended grey beard; navy waistcoat, cream sleeves, burgundy scarf, brass keys; ledger and quill.

**Consistency:** Not Elder: no walking stick, bald crown or flowing white beard. Not Silver: no cropped hair, square worker beard or sleeveless apron.
